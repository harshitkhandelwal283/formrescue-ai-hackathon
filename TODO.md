## 4. Evidence, diagnostic report and recovery checklist

- [x] Provide an evidence-strength/coverage indicator based on a documented coverage rubric, state that it is indicative and not a statistically validated probability, and communicate remaining uncertainty. A high indicator must not override missing/contradictory evidence.
